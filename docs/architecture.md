# Mercury OS Architecture

Mercury OS is organized around independent evidence layers so high-risk opportunities remain aggressive only when multiple systems agree.

## Core layers

1. Market regime and Maximum Market Outlook
2. Gem discovery and quiet accumulation
3. Catalyst and filing intelligence
4. Social and promotion network radar
5. Liquidity and microstructure
6. Calculated aggression scoring
7. Independent risk gateway
8. Portfolio capacity and exposure
9. Exit and peak-pressure engine
10. Replay, attribution, and model learning
11. Audit, workflow, and model governance

## Autonomous mode

Autonomous mode researches, enriches, scores, ranks, blocks, alerts, and records decisions. Capital execution is disabled until a separately governed execution phase is approved.

## Scheduling

A one-minute Vercel cron pulse dispatches only jobs due at the current UTC minute. This avoids separate overlapping cron endpoints and gives one auditable scheduler.

## Data warehouse

Postgres stores securities, market snapshots, social observations, filings, share structures, corporate actions, opportunities, workflow runs, and decision logs.

## Safety hierarchy

Hard structural risk > execution/liquidity risk > peak risk > portfolio risk > alpha score.

A high alpha score never overrides a hard block.
