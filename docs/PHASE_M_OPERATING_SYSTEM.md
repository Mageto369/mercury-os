# Mercury OS — Phase M Operating System

Mercury remains research/shadow only. Capital execution is disabled.

## H — Alpha Factory 2.0
Live-only signal attribution measures hit rate, expectancy, correlation and signal decay. Weak signals remain shadow or are retired; candidates require evidence.

## I — Regime Ensemble
`regime_states` and `model_ensemble_scores` provide the storage contract for regime-specific champion/challenger research and model disagreement.

## J — Portfolio Brain
`portfolio_decisions` stores simulated portfolio-level decisions, concentration, liquidity capacity and marginal-risk evidence. It has no broker authority.

## K — Historical Laboratory
`research_experiments`, `proof_metrics`, and `replay_evidence` separate point-in-time, walk-forward, leakage and transaction-cost proof from live authoritative evidence.

## L — 24/7 Shadow Production
The production loop is ingest → validate → graph → deep intelligence → score → risk → outcomes → attribution → shadow portfolio. Continuous operation must not be claimed until production hosting, runtime secrets, providers and cron execution are verified.

## M — Economic Proof Gate
`economic_proof_gates` is LIVE EVIDENCE ONLY. The initial hard gate requires at least 1,000 matured live 60-minute outcomes, positive expectancy, positive expectancy after removing the top 1% winners, and max drawdown no worse than -25%. These are minimum engineering gates, not proof of future profitability.

`paper_orders` is simulation-only. Passing Phase M can make autonomous paper simulation eligible, but never enables real-money execution. Real capital remains outside this architecture and requires a separate explicit governance and risk decision.
